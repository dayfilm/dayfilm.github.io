Support

Need help with Dayfilm, found a bug, or have an idea for a film look? Email dayfilm.app@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if it is about a problem.

Common questions

Where are my photos?
Every photo you take is saved to the Photos app on your iPhone. Dayfilm does not keep its own library.

How does the free trial work?
Tap "Try Free for 7 Days" in the app. For 7 days you get every film look and feature. When it ends, the Classic look stays free and you can unlock everything with a one-time purchase.

I already paid but the app is locked.
Open Settings inside Dayfilm and tap "Restore Purchase". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Can I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

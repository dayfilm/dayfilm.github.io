Support

Need help with Dayfilm, found a bug, or have an idea for a filter? Email dayfilm.app@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if it is about a problem.

Common questions

Where are my photos?
Every photo you take is saved to the Photos app on your iPhone. If it can't be saved there, for example because Photos access is off, it stays in the app: open the Files app and look under Dayfilm › Captures.

How does the free trial work?
Tap "Try Free for 7 Days" in the app. For 7 days you get every filter and effect. When it ends, the camera's own colors, black and white, grain and vignette stay free, and a one-time purchase unlocks the three filters, the date and Develop.

I already paid but the app is locked.
Open Dayfilm's Settings (the sliders icon at the top right of the camera screen) and tap RESTORE under Full Version, or tap any locked filter and choose "Restore Purchase". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Can I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Dayfilm is a film camera app for iPhone. This policy explains how Dayfilm handles your information. The short version: Dayfilm does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Dayfilm has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet to send any information about you or your use of the app.

Camera and photos

Dayfilm asks for permission to use the camera so you can take photos, and for access to your Photos library so it can save what you shoot and so Develop can open photos you've already taken. All processing, including subject and depth detection in Develop, happens on your device. Photos are saved to your own Photos library, or kept inside the app on your device if Photos access is off. Develop saves a new photo and does not change your originals. Dayfilm never uploads your photos anywhere.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Dayfilm does not see or store your payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Settings

Your in-app preferences, such as the selected filter, are stored locally on your device and are not shared.

Children

Dayfilm does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If Dayfilm ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: dayfilm.app@gmail.com
